About the Project

Many older adults undergo surgery each year, and some are at elevated risk of postoperative cognitive complications such as delirium. Brief, reliable cognitive screening before surgery can help care teams identify those at risk. PRECEDE II builds on our earlier work using the digital Clock Drawing Test, a quick pen-and-paper style task captured with a digital pen, to assess cognition in older adults preparing for surgery.

A central focus of PRECEDE II is educational heterogeneity. Standard screening tools and classifiers can mistake lower formal education for cognitive impairment. We develop explainable machine learning methods that work for all patients, regardless of educational background.

In 2026 the project received a five-year, $3.7 million renewal award from the National Institutes of Health, supporting the work through 2031. Read the UF announcement →

Specific Aims

  1. Explainable AI-enhanced digital Clock Drawing Test

    Develop interpretable deep learning models that extract clinically meaningful features from digital clock drawings.

  2. Postoperative outcome prediction

    Build predictive models that combine cognitive and surgical data to improve prediction of postoperative outcomes.

  3. Equitable screening across education levels

    Evaluate alternative digital cognitive screening approaches for people with lower formal education.

Data Availability

The data used in PRECEDE II were collected from human participants and contain protected health information. To protect participant privacy, the datasets are not publicly released.

De-identified data are available upon reasonable request. Requests are reviewed to ensure compliance with the National Institutes of Health (NIH) data sharing policies, the University of Florida Institutional Review Board (IRB), and the terms of participant consent. A data use agreement may be required.

Please direct all data requests to Dr. Catherine Price (cep23@phhp.ufl.edu). Include your name, affiliation, intended use of the data, and the specific variables or modalities requested.
